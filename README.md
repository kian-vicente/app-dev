My Favorite Series: Stranger Things
 
Introduction
 
My favorite series is Stranger Things. It is a thrilling sci‑fi and mystery series about a group of friends in a small town.
 
I like this series because it is suspenseful, fun, and full of unexpected adventures.
 
About the Series
 
Stranger Things tells the story of kids from Hawkins, Indiana, as they uncover strange secrets and dangerous otherworldly forces.
 
Main Characters
 
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers
 
Why I Like It
 
I enjoy watching this series because:
 
1. The adventure is very exciting.
2. The friendship between the kids is heartwarming.
3. The monsters and supernatural parts are cool.
 
My Favorite Quote
 
Friends don’t lie.
 
My Rating
 
9/10 - I recommend this series to people who love sci‑fi and mystery.
